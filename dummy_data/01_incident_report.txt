1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
On 17 April 2026 at 02:14 UTC, the Security Operations Center (SOC) detected an anomalous authentication event targeting the primary Research Portal infrastructure. An unauthorized session was established using the compromised privileged account 'admin-research' originating from external IP 185.203.117.42. 

Subsequent activity resulted in degraded availability and administrative lockout across core services, causing approximately 47 minutes of operational disruption to the external Research Portal before containment protocols isolated the session.

2. AFFECTED SYSTEMS & INFRASTRUCTURE
The following internal assets were confirmed affected during the incident window:
- PORTAL-01: Public-facing Research Web Gateway (HTTP/HTTPS services degraded)
- APP-02: Research Application & Processing Backend (unauthorized command execution detected)
- AUTH-01: Central Identity Directory & SSO Service (credential replay / token generation)

3. IMPACT ASSESSMENT
- Operational Impact: Research portal experienced approximately 47 minutes of disruption (02:35 UTC to 03:22 UTC) until emergency routing and isolation were implemented.
- Data Integrity: Preliminary forensic integrity checks indicate no database alteration; evidence of unauthorized read access to non-classified research indexes.
- Severity Level: SEVERITY-HIGH (Tier 2 Cyber Incident)

4. CURRENT STATUS & CONTAINMENT
- Account 'admin-research' has been revoked, session tokens invalidated, and credentials reset.
- IP 185.203.117.42 blocked at perimeter firewall and cloud CDN edge.
- Service restored on PORTAL-01 and APP-02 as of 03:22 UTC.
- Enhanced telemetry and packet capture active across AUTH-01.

5. ATTRIBUTION STATUS
ATTRIBUTION IS CURRENTLY UNCONFIRMED.
While initial indicators overlap with known commodity scanning patterns, no definitive attribution to any specific Advanced Persistent Threat (APT) group or known threat actor has been established. Investigators emphasize that attribution remains strictly UNCONFIRMED at this time.
